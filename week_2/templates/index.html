Four Models, One App

Sentiment analysis, text summarization, speech-to-text and image captioning — four pretrained models on your own input.

1 · Sentiment analysis

your text → label  |  pretrained DistilBERT






  
2 · Text summarization

  
your text → summary  |  pretrained DistilBART (CNN/DailyMail)

  
  
  






  
3 · Speech to text

  
your voice or audio file → text  |  pretrained Whisper (tiny.en)

  
  
  
Click the mic and speak in English, or choose an audio file.






  
4 · Image captioning

  
your image → caption  |  pretrained BLIP

  
  
  
  
Choose an image, then click Caption.
